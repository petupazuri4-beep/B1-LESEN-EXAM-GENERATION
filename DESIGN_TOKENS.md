# B1 Lesen Exam Studio – Design Tokens & Visual Architecture

## 1. Typography & Hierarchy
- **Primary Body Font**: `Source Sans 3`, `Open Sans`, system sans-serif (8–15 words per sentence target for B1 reading levels).
- **Secondary Serif Option**: `Merriweather`, `Georgia` for authentic press/magazine layout emulation.
- **Monospace Code & Numbers**: `JetBrains Mono` for item identifiers (`#01–#30`), raw scores, timing countdowns (`65:00`), and candidate IDs.
- **Line Heights**:
  - Regular Text: `1.45` to `1.65`
  - Accessible Reader: `1.85` to `2.2`

## 2. Color Palette & Themes
- **Goethe Grün**: Primary `#15803d` / `#166534`, Accent Amber `#d97706`
- **ÖSD Rot**: Primary `#b91c1c` / `#991b1b`, Badge Ruby `#e11d48`
- **Alpen Blau**: Primary `#0369a1` / `#075985`, Accent Cyan `#0891b2`
- **Berlin Nacht**: Primary `#334155` / `#0f172a`, Accent Violet `#7c3aed`
- **Wien Klassik**: Primary `#78350f` / `#451a03`, Accent Gold `#b45309`

## 3. Examination Grid Standards
- **A4 Dimensions**: `210mm x 297mm`
- **Margins**: Normal (`20mm`), Narrow (`12mm`), Wide (`25mm`)
- **Zeilennummerierung**: Every 5 lines (`5, 10, 15, 20...`) in margin gutter
- **Note Lines (Entwurf)**: 4 dotted/dashed lines with 8mm rule height

## 4. CEFR B1 Pass Criteria
- **Raw Score Maximum**: 30 points
- **Scaled Score Maximum**: 100 points
- **Pass Threshold**: 60 points (minimum 18 correct answers out of 30)
